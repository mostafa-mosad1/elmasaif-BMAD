updated: 2026-10-26T15:47

- (decision) Platform scope: Real estate sales marketplace exclusively for Saudi Arabia (KSA); rentals strictly excluded
- (decision) Core interaction model: Mandatory mediation where all inquiries, offers, and chats pass through admin mediation; direct communication prohibited before deal completion
- (decision) Financial flow: Buyer pays property price directly to Seller; Buyer pays platform commission to platform; platform never holds property funds; two payment proofs reviewed within 48h deadline
- (decision) Seller compliance: Marketers require approved, unexpired Fal license; Owners require approved request, signed electronic contract, and verified contract fee proof before publication
- (decision) Tech stack: Next.js frontend (presentation only) plus NestJS modular monolith REST API with Prisma, PostgreSQL, and pg-boss queue; Arabic RTL first
- (direction) Execution roadmap: Epic E0 (platform foundation) is the initial technical prerequisite across monorepo packages (apps/web, apps/api, packages/shared)
- (event) Product Brief draft completed with addendum and memlog audit
