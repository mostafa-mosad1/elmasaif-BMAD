# Story 4.2: Mediated Counter-Offer Rounds & Seller Responses

Status: review

<!-- Note: Validation is optional. Run validate-create-story for quality check before dev-story. -->

## Story

As a **seller receiving a purchase offer**,
I want to **accept, reject, or submit a counter-offer through administrative mediation**,
so that **negotiation proceeds constructively with administrative oversight up to a 5-round limit.**

This story realizes **FR-26 (Mediated Counter-Offer Rounds)**, **FR-55 (Seller Response to Mediated Offers)**, the mediation half of **UX-DR-10 / UX-DR-13**, and the `Offer` half of **AD-10** (claim-locked queues). It is the **second** story of Epic 4 and the middle of the offer pipeline:

- **Story 4.1 (done)** left the offer parked in `PENDING_ADMIN_REVIEW` with `// TODO(4.2): relay to seller / admin mediation queue` markers.
- **Story 4.2 (this)** owns: the `Offers.Review` admin mediation queue with 15-minute claim locking, the relay to the seller, the seller's accept/reject/counter responses, the counter-offer ping-pong back through admin to the buyer, the 5-round cap, the `SelectedPendingFinalAdminApproval` hand-off, and the 7-day expiry sweep.
- **Story 4.3** owns backup-queue activation surfacing; **Story 5.1** owns turning `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL` into a `Deal`.

The `Offer` model, `OfferHistory` model, the `offers` API module (2 endpoints), the buyer offers UI, and `ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES` / `TERMINAL_OFFER_STATUSES` / `OFFER_STATUS_LABELS_AR` all **already exist** from 4.1. Every change in this story is **additive** — 4.1's contract, components and tests must keep passing.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Admin mediation relay (FR-26, AD-10, UX `/admin/negotiations/:id`):** an admin holding `Offers.Review` relays an offer awaiting mediation via `POST /api/v1/admin/offers/:id/relay-to-seller` → `PENDING_ADMIN_REVIEW` → `RELAYED_TO_SELLER`, `expiresAt` reset to `ClockService.now() + OFFER_EXPIRY_HOURS`, an `OfferHistory` row (`OFFER_RELAYED_TO_SELLER`) and an `AuditLog` row written in the same transaction, and the admin's claim lock cleared on commit. Only `PENDING_ADMIN_REVIEW` is relayable → otherwise **409** `OFFER_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`.
2. **Seller responses (FR-55):** an authenticated `MARKETER`/`OWNER` whose **active role profile owns the listing** can respond to an offer in `RELAYED_TO_SELLER`:
   - `POST /api/v1/offers/:id/accept-by-seller` → `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL` (mutual agreement: the buyer already agreed to their own price).
   - `POST /api/v1/offers/:id/reject-by-seller` with a **mandatory structured reason** (`PRICE_TOO_LOW | FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE | PROPERTY_UNAVAILABLE | TERMS_MISMATCH | OTHER`; `OTHER` requires a note ≥ 10 chars) → `REJECTED_BY_SELLER` (terminal).
   - `POST /api/v1/offers/:id/counter-by-seller` with `counterPrice` + optional note → `COUNTER_OFFERED`, `negotiationRound + 1`, `Offer.offerPrice` replaced by the counter price, previous price preserved in `OfferHistory.priceSnapshot`.
   - Any other source status → **409** `OFFER_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`; a caller who is not the listing's seller → **403** `OFFER_NOT_PARTY`; a caller with no active `MARKETER`/`OWNER` profile → **403** `OFFER_SELLER_PROFILE_REQUIRED`.
3. **Counter-offer round cap (FR-26):** `MAX_COUNTER_ROUNDS = 5`. A counter-offer is refused with **409** `OFFER_MAX_ROUNDS_REACHED` once `negotiationRound >= MAX_COUNTER_ROUNDS`; the cap applies identically to buyer and seller counters, and `accept` is always still allowed at the cap.
4. **Counter-offers are mediated back to the buyer (FR-26, UX "approve or reject terms before relaying"):** `POST /api/v1/admin/offers/:id/relay-to-buyer` moves `COUNTER_OFFERED` → `RELAYED_TO_BUYER`; the buyer then either `POST /offers/:id/accept-by-buyer` → `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL` or `POST /offers/:id/counter-by-buyer` → `COUNTER_OFFERED` with `negotiationRound + 1`. Direct un-mediated seller↔buyer contact remains impossible: no endpoint exposes the counterparty's mobile number, email, or IBAN.
5. **Mutual agreement hand-off (FR-26):** the moment either party accepts the currently proposed price the offer becomes `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL`, the admin lock is cleared, and the offer leaves the mediation queue. `Deal` creation is **not** in this story (`// TODO(5.1)`).
6. **Admin rejection of mediated terms:** `POST /api/v1/admin/offers/:id/reject` with a mandatory `reason` (5–500 chars) moves `PENDING_ADMIN_REVIEW` or `COUNTER_OFFERED` → `REJECTED_BY_ADMIN` (terminal) with history + audit rows.
7. **Seven-day expiry sweep (FR-26, AD-7 job #2):** the `offer-expiry-checker` job transitions every offer with `status ∈ EXPIRABLE_OFFER_STATUSES` and `expiresAt <= now` to `EXPIRED`, writing an `OfferHistory` (`OFFER_EXPIRED`) and a `SYSTEM` `AuditLog` row. `EXPIRABLE_OFFER_STATUSES` is `ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES` minus `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL`: a mutually-agreed offer is **answered**, not unanswered, so it must never be auto-killed while Story 5.1 is binding it into a `Deal` (the same exclusion `demand-expiry-checker` already applies to selected proposals). It is **idempotent** (an `EXPIRED` offer is never re-processed) and unit-tested through an injected DB client — the exact factory shape used by `demand-expiry-checker` in 3.6.
8. **Negotiation timeline (UX-DR-10, UX-DR-13):** a vertical chronological thread renders every `OfferHistory` entry in order (actor, round chip, price snapshot, note, Saudi-time timestamp) on the offer detail screen, with the current ask / current proposed price / round counter ("الجولة X من 5") and the mediation transparency badge «كافة المفاوضات تتم تحت إشراف المنصة…». On mobile the party's actions live in a **sticky bottom action tray** («قبول العرض» emerald, «تقديم عرض مقابل جديد» outlined); on desktop they render inline. The view short-polls the thread every 20 s while the offer is non-terminal.
9. **Admin mediation queue with 15-minute claim locking (AD-10):** `GET /api/v1/admin/offers` lists offers awaiting mediation (`PENDING_ADMIN_REVIEW`, `COUNTER_OFFERED`) oldest-first with pagination, lock info, and lock-holder names. `POST /admin/offers/:id/claim|heartbeat|release` implement the 15-minute lease with lazy expiry; `POST /admin/offers/:id/force-unlock` requires `Tasks.ForceUnlock` and a documented reason ≥ 5 chars. Claiming is an explicit `POST`, never a `GET`; a second admin sees read-only mode with the holder's name.
10. **Seller surface exists and is reachable:** `GET /api/v1/offers/received` returns the offers on the caller's own listings (newest first) and backs a new `/app/marketer/offers` and `/app/owner/offers` page pair, both linked from the workspace sidebar under «العروض الواردة». `GET /api/v1/offers/:id` returns the full thread (listing summary, parties' display names, history, round state) to **either party only**; a third party gets **403** `OFFER_NOT_PARTY`, an unknown id **404** `OFFER_NOT_FOUND`.
11. **Auditable, deterministic state changes (AD-7, AD-11):** every transition above writes `OfferHistory` **and** `AuditLog` inside the same `prisma.$transaction`, guarded by a row lock on the offer (`$queryRaw ... FOR UPDATE` with a `::text` cast — `offers.id` is a `text` column). Every timestamp comes from `ClockService.now()`; `Date.now()` and arg-less `new Date()` are forbidden in services and jobs.
12. **Listings coherence (FR-14 regression guard):** the four hard-coded "open offer" status literals in the listings module are replaced with `[...ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES]` from `@elmasaif/shared` so archive / compliance-suspend / compliance-block stay correct for every new status, and the co-located spec that asserts those arrays is updated to assert the same constant.
13. **No regressions:** listings/search/map/detail/favorites, demands, proposals, geo, auth, admin auth, and the worker job registry keep their behaviour. `packages/shared` changes are additive except the `counterOfferSchema` stub, which is relocated (public name and `CounterOfferInput` preserved). `POST /offers`, `GET /offers/my`, `OfferItem`, `BuyerOffersList` and the 4.1 web specs keep working unchanged.

### Critical implementation traps (read before coding)
- **🔴 `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL` does not exist in `OfferStatus`.** It exists only in `ProposalStatus`. Add it to **both** the Prisma enum and `packages/shared/src/enums/index.ts` in the same commit, or `Offer.status` can never reach the state Epic 5 keys off. Note the CRUD-created `offer.update` payload is a plain string union — a missing enum member is a compile error only if you import the enum (do).
- **🔴 `offers.id` is a `text` column, not `uuid`.** 4.1 shipped a runtime 500 (`P2010` / `42883: operator does not exist: text = uuid`) because a Dev Note said `::uuid`. Every `$queryRaw ... FOR UPDATE` in this story must cast `::text`. Keep 4.1's regression assertion alive and add the same assertion for the new lock helper.
- **🔴 `OfferHistory` has no `actorAdminId`.** 4.1 wrote `actorProfileId` (a `UserProfile` id) for buyer actions. Admin actions have no `UserProfile` — without `actorAdminId` you cannot tell an admin relay from a seller action, and the timeline would mislabel every admin step as «البائع». Add the column.
- **🔴 The four "open offer" literal arrays are already inconsistent** (the archive guard includes `ACCEPTED_BY_*` but not `COUNTER_OFFERED`; the withdraw arrays include `COUNTER_OFFERED` but not `ACCEPTED_BY_*`). Do **not** hand-extend them with the new statuses — replace all four with `[...ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES]`, or `RELAYED_TO_BUYER` / `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL` offers will be silently orphaned when a listing is archived or blocked. `listings-compliance-archive.spec.ts` asserts the array **literally** — update it with the code.
- **🔴 `expiresAt` must be reset on every non-terminal transition.** If it is only set at submission, a healthy 3-round negotiation dies on day 7 from the *original* timestamp and the sweep kills a live conversation. The 7-day clock measures the current round.
- **🔴 Do not mutate `offerPrice` without preserving history.** `offerPrice` becomes the *current* proposal on each counter; the replaced price must already be in `OfferHistory.priceSnapshot` (it is, from 4.1's `OFFER_SUBMITTED` and each subsequent counter). Never `update` the Offer before writing the history row in the same transaction.
- **🔴 `packages/shared/dist` is what jest/vitest resolve.** Run `cd packages/shared && npx tsc` **before** any api/web/worker test run (bit Stories 3.4–4.1).
- **🔴 The claim must be a conditional `updateMany`, not read-then-`update`.** `admin-listings.service.ts` reads first, then updates — a race two concurrent admins can lose. AD-10 mandates the atomic conditional `UPDATE`; `count === 0` is the only reliable conflict signal.

- **🔴 New enum members break the 4.1 shared invariant test** ("`ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES` and `TERMINAL_OFFER_STATUSES` are disjoint and together cover the enum"). Update both arrays; do not delete the test.
- **🔴 `GET /offers/:id` must be declared after `GET /offers/my` and `GET /offers/received`.** NestJS matches in declaration order; a `:id` route declared first swallows both literals.
- **🔴 No committed migrations.** `npm run prisma:push` + `npm run prisma:generate` in `apps/api`; inspect the diff and reject anything destructive against the seeded dev DB.

### Architecture compliance (MUST follow — ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md)
- **AD-1 / AD-2:** the web app never touches Prisma; every read/write goes through `apiClient` over the same-origin `/api` proxy. Transitions are semantic `POST` actions (`/accept-by-seller`, `/counter-by-buyer`, `/relay-to-seller`) — **no generic `PATCH`, no Server Actions**.
- **AD-4 / AD-8:** each offer mutation runs in one `prisma.$transaction` with a `$queryRaw ... FOR UPDATE` row lock on the offer before any state check, so two racing responses cannot both win.
- **AD-7 / AD-11:** `OfferHistory` + `AuditLog` written in the same transaction as the state change; every instant from `ClockService.now()` (no `new Date()` as a time source; `new Date(now.getTime() + …)` arithmetic is fine). The expiry sweep takes its `currentTime` from the worker clock.
- **AD-10:** `Offer` is one of the 8 claim-locked queues. Claim is an explicit `POST`, 15-minute lease, 5-minute heartbeat, lazy expiry, read-only banner for other admins, `Tasks.ForceUnlock` with a mandatory reason, lock released on decision commit. A claim never replaces a version check — this repo has no `If-Match`, documented rather than invented.
- **Real-time convention:** "Short polling (15–30s) for active negotiations" — the detail view polls at **20 s**, matching the architecture's stated band.
- **NFR1:** every guardrail (round cap, state machine, party check, expiry) is enforced **server-side**; the sticky tray is a UX affordance only.
- **Stack (pinned):** NestJS 10.x + Prisma 5.x, Next.js App Router + React 19 + Tailwind 3.4, Zod 3.23, TypeScript strict. **No new dependencies.**

### Decision log (make these calls, do not re-litigate in code)
- **`ACCEPTED_BY_SELLER` / `ACCEPTED_BY_BUYER` / `SUBMITTED` / `IN_REVIEW` stay in the enum but are never produced by this story.** Acceptance goes straight to `SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL`, because FR-55 and the epic AC both say seller acceptance *is* mutual agreement. Two "accepted" states would need translating at every boundary, and removing enum members is a destructive schema change on a seeded DB. They remain in `ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES` so they can never be treated as dead offers.
- **A counter-offer is re-mediated in both directions**, so `RELAYED_TO_BUYER` is added alongside `RELAYED_TO_SELLER`. Reusing `COUNTER_OFFERED` for both directions would force `awaitingParty` to guess from round parity — an ambiguity the sticky tray cannot survive.
- **`REJECTED_BY_ADMIN` is a real terminal status**, not a `WITHDRAWN` rename: UX requires "approve or reject terms before relaying", and the buyer/seller must be able to tell an administrative veto from their own counterparty's rejection.
- **The structured rejection reason is a Zod enum stored in a `String?` column**, not a new Prisma enum: the value is display/analytics-only, `OfferHistory.action` is already a `String`, and avoiding a new Postgres type keeps `db push` strictly additive.
- **`expiresAt` resets on every non-terminal transition** (7 days per round, not 7 days per thread). FR-26 states the expiry, not that it is anchored to submission.
- **`SELECTED_PENDING_FINAL_ADMIN_APPROVAL` stays in `ACTIVE_OFFER_STATUSES` but is excluded from `EXPIRABLE_OFFER_STATUSES`.** It must remain "active" for the listings archive/suspend/block guards and the buyer dashboard counter, but it is not awaiting a party response, so the 7-day sweep must skip it. Story 5.1 owns its next transition.
- **The mediation decision endpoints require the caller to hold the claim lock.** This is stricter than `admin-listings.approve`, but AD-10 ("lock is released on decision commit") only makes sense if the decision validates the lease. `409` codes reuse the listings semantics.
- **The 7-day sweep ships as a testable factory whose handler is registered without a DB client** — the same shape 3.6 accepted for `demand-expiry-checker` and 1.9 for `account-deletion-expiry`. The worker has no Prisma dependency and no job in this repo is runtime-DB-wired yet; inventing a second data-access path inside the worker would be a larger, riskier change than this story's scope. The logic is fully unit-tested through the injected client and is the only piece a later infra story must wire.
- **One shared `OfferNegotiationView` mounted at three thin route files** (`/app/buyer/offers/[id]`, `/app/marketer/offers/[id]`, `/app/owner/offers/[id]`) instead of a role-agnostic `/app/offers/[id]`: `EXPERIENCE.md` specifies `/app/buyer/offers/:id`, and each workspace must stay inside its own tree so the sidebar highlights correctly. The API — not the route — is the authority on party scope.
- **`OfferItem` gains only optional fields** (`negotiationRound`, `awaitingParty`). Making them required would break 4.1's `submitOffer` mapper contract and its SSR specs for no benefit.
- **No `Idempotency-Key` / `If-Match` enforcement.** Neither exists repo-wide; a retried decision is naturally rejected by the state machine (`OFFER_INVALID_STATE_TRANSITION`) once the first one commits. `version` is incremented on every transition so a later story can enforce it.
- **`GET /api/v1/admin/offers/:id` is allowed** if the admin workspace needs a single-item fetch — an additive read on the admin module, not a scope expansion.
